WITH LOVE YOUR BROTHER
PRIVATE MESSAGE FINAL ARCHIVE

Agar tum ye message padh rahi ho…

toh iska matlab hai ke tum meri banayi hui is chhoti si duniya ke bilkul aakhri kone tak pahunch gayi ho. ❤️

Aur honestly…

shayad ye woh part hai jo mere liye sabse mushkil tha.

Kyunkay birthday wish karna easy hai.

“Happy Birthday.” “Stay happy.” “Allah tumhein hamesha khush rakhe.”

Ye sab kehna easy hai.

Lekin jo baat dil mein hoti hai na…

usay words mein likhna itna easy nahi hota.

Tum mujhse sirf ek saal chhoti ho.

Lekin kabhi kabhi mujhe lagta hai jaise humne saath mein ek poori zindagi dekhi hai.

Woh choti choti laraiyan… ek dusre ko irritate karna… bina wajah hansna… ek dusre ki taang kheenchna… aur woh moments jinhein us waqt humne kabhi special samjha hi nahi.

Aaj peeche mud kar dekhta hoon toh samajh aata hai…

shayad wohi moments sabse special thay.

Tum shayad kabhi notice na karo, lekin tumhari khushi dekh kar mujhe andar se ek alag hi sukoon milta hai.

Aur agar kabhi tumhari life mein koi aisa waqt aaye jab tumhein lage ke tum akeli ho…

toh bas itna yaad rakhna:

Tum akeli nahi ho.

Chahe waqt badal jaye. Chahe hum dono apni apni lifes mein kitne bhi busy ho jayein. Chahe kal hum aaj ki tarah har roz baat na karein…

tum meri chhoti behen ho, aur ye rishta kabhi old nahi hoga.

18 October 2003…

shayad duniya ke liye bas ek date thi.

Lekin mere liye us din meri life mein ek aisi person add hui, jiski importance mujhe waqt ke saath aur zyada samajh aayi.

Aur aaj…

18 October 2026.

Tum 23 ki ho gayi ho.

23 saal…

Aur mujhe bas ek hi dua karni hai:

Allah tumhari zindagi ko itni khubsurat bana de ke tumhein apne guzre hue kal ka kabhi afsos na ho, aur apne aane wale kal ka kabhi darr na lage.

Tumhare dreams poore hon. Tumhara dil hamesha halka rahe. Tumhein woh log milen jo tumhari value samjhein. Aur jab life mushkil ho…

Allah tumhein har baar us mushkil se pehle se zyada strong bana kar nikale.

Main shayad tumhein roz nahi bolunga ke tum mere liye important ho.

Shayad kabhi kabhi main tumhein annoy bhi karunga. Kabhi humari larai bhi hogi. Kabhi tum mujhse naraz hogi. Kabhi main tumse.

Lekin in sab cheezon ke peeche ek cheez hamesha same rahegi:

Main tumhari khushi ke liye hamesha dua karunga.

Aur agar kabhi tum ye socho ke…

“Bhai ko meri fikr bhi hai?”

Toh is message ko dobara padhna.

Kyunkay shayad main har baar keh nahi pata…

lekin haan, bohot hai. ❤️

Happy 23rd Birthday, meri chhoti behen.

Allah tumhein lambi, khubsurat, peaceful aur kamyab zindagi de.

Aur please…

kabhi apni smile mat khona.

Kyunkay tumhari smile sirf tumhari nahi hai…

kuch logon ki khushi bhi usmein chhupi hoti hai.

— Tumhara Bhai ❤️
